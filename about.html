Our Brand Story: Nutrition, Science, Love

Lolo Treats was founded by a team of veterinarians and food scientists who understood that fresh, whole foods are key to a long, happy dog life. Our mission goes beyond convenience; it's about pioneering canine wellness.

Rooted in Expertise

Every recipe is formulated by veterinary nutritionists (DACVN) to meet AAFCO standards. We analyze your pet's needs and create balanced, bespoke portions, eliminating the guesswork from their diet.

A Commitment to Quality

We source only human-grade ingredients from local, trusted suppliers. Our food is gently cooked, ensuring maximum nutrient retention and palatability. We are passionate about the health outcomes, not just the profits.
